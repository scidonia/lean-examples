# Outlook

## What the models establish, and what they do not
- Proved: the permission discipline for any lawful type, detached reads in the modelled encoding, snapshots, frames, and the universe's laws for every non-recursive description.
- Not proved: Lean's physical pointers. `NoRef` speaks of the encoding; a dishonest encoder is outside the theorem. The `inplace` harness remains the run-time evidence.
- Next: recursive descriptions, path reads and take-out writes for arrays of structs, and a real mutable cell behind the sealed interface.
